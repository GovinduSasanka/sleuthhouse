Parents’ Secret Briefing

The Case of the Vanished Cookies · Junior Inspector (Ages 8–10) · about 30–45 minutes · Case no. SAMPLE-1

Setup (10 minutes)

  1. Print every page. Keep this page and the Solution page away from the detectives.
  2. Cut out the clue cards and suspect cards along the dashed lines.
  3. Hide clue cards 1 to 5 in the spots in the table below. Folding them in half helps.
  4. Hand the detectives the Mission Letter, the suspect cards, the Detective Notebook and a pencil.
  5. Read the Mission Letter out loud, dramatically. Then let the hunt begin!
  6. When they’re ready, they fill in the Accusation. Check it against the Solution and read the confession out loud.

Good to know

  • Every clue is true. The case was checked by a logic solver: there is exactly one possible thief, and no card gives it away early.
  • Detectives only need the clues. Nothing depends on luck or guessing.
  • Players in the case can play too! The suspects are only pretend.
  • Stuck detectives? Read out a hint from the right-hand column of the table below.

🍪 The Case of the Vanished Cookies

Dear Junior Inspector Nila & Kavin,

We have a serious situation in your house. At exactly 3:15 this afternoon, someone lifted the lid of the cookie jar, and the last chocolate-chip cookies disappeared. Only crumbs and a guilty silence remain.

The crime happened in the Living Room. Our investigation shows that the thief must be one of these 5 suspects:

👩 Mum · 👨 Dad · 👵 Grandma · 🐶 Biscuit · 🧸 Mr Fluff

I have hidden 5 clue cards around the house. Each clue card tells you something true about the case and shows you where to find the next one. Use your Detective Notebook to cross off suspects as you go.

When you have found every card and only one suspect is left, fill in the Accusation form. Choose carefully. A good detective never guesses!

The whole house is counting on you.

  3. By elimination → Dad was drawing pictures.
  4. Clue card 5 → Mum can't be the thief.
  5. Clue card 5 → Biscuit can't be the thief.
  6. Clue card 5 → Mr Fluff can't be the thief.
  7. Only Dad is left, so Dad is the thief!
