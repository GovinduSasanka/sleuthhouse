Most printable mysteries are written once and sold to thousands of families. AI-written ones can contradict themselves. Sleuthhouse works differently.

  • A logic engine builds each case. It invents what really happened, then chooses clues until reasoning alone points to one thief.
  • No clue gives it away early. The engine removes every clue the solution doesn’t need, so detectives need every card.
  • Parents get the proof. The secret sheet walks through the solution step by step, so you can give hints without spoiling it.
  • Private by design. Cases are built in your browser. Your family’s names never leave your device.

    Questions

    What do I need?

    A printer (black & white is fine), scissors, a pencil and about 10 minutes to hide the clue cards. No app, no account.

    Can a grown-up who is a suspect still play?

    Yes! Nobody really did it. The suspects are just characters. The parent who hides the clues should keep the secret sheet to themselves.

    Can we play again?

    Every case number makes a completely new mystery with a new thief, new clues and new hiding spots. Roll a new number and play again.

    How do you know it’s solvable?

    Each case is checked by a deduction solver before you see it. It only reasons the way a person can, so if the solver can name exactly one thief using the clues, so can your detectives. We also test thousands of cases by brute force.

    Are the hiding spots safe?

    Our built-in spots avoid ovens, washing machines, medicine cabinets and high shelves. You can also add your own.

    Is my family’s information uploaded?

    No. Cases are generated entirely in your browser.
