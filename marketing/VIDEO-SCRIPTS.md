# Short video scripts (TikTok · Reels · Shorts)

Film vertical (9:16), keep each under 45 seconds, and put the site link in your bio. Always get parents’ permission before filming any child, and never show a child’s full name, school or address. Real play footage works far better than polished ads.

---

## 1. “I turned our house into a crime scene”

**Hook (0–2s, text on screen):** *I turned our house into a crime scene for my 7-year-old.*

**Shots:**
1. Close-up: an empty cookie jar with one crumb. Text: *The cookies are GONE.*
2. A kid reading the mission letter, eyes wide.
3. Fast cuts: lifting a sofa cushion → finding a clue card → running to the next room.
4. The kid pointing dramatically at Dad.
5. Dad reads the confession: “In my defence, the cookies looked lonely.”

**On-screen text at the end:** *Every case is different. Your family are the suspects.*

**Caption:** Rainy afternoon sorted 🔍 The suspects were our own family (the dog was very nervous). Printable mystery link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #rainydayactivities #kidsactivities #screenfreekids #parentinghacks #momtok #dadtok

---

## 2. “The dog was the prime suspect”

**Hook:** *The evidence pointed at the dog.* 🐶

**Shots:**
1. The dog’s suspect card on the table (“Known for: can hear a snack bag open from three rooms away”).
2. The kid reading a clue: “A trail of popcorn crumbs. That means the thief was munching something crunchy.”
3. Slow zoom on the dog looking guilty.
4. Plot twist: the final clue clears the dog. The kid gasps.
5. The real thief (Grandma) confesses.

**Caption:** Justice for Biscuit. 🐾 He was innocent the whole time.

**Hashtags:** #dogsoftiktok #kidsactivities #familygamenight #mystery

---

## 3. “Can you crack this code?” (interactive)

**Hook:** *My 9-year-old cracked this in 40 seconds. Can you?*

**Shots:**
1. Close-up of a clue card with the emoji code 🐧 🍦 🍋 🍋 🐙 🐋 and the emoji key.
2. A 3-second pause with a countdown timer on screen.
3. The kid writing letters in the boxes: P-I-L-L-O-W.
4. The kid sprinting to a bedroom and pulling the next card from under a pillow.

**Caption:** Comment your answer before the reveal 👀 (The codes get harder at the Master Sleuth level: pigpen cipher!)

**Hashtags:** #puzzle #riddle #kidsactivities #homeschool #brainteaser

---

## 4. “Halloween party game that isn’t scary”

**Hook:** *Halloween game for kids that isn’t scary (but is SO fun).*

**Shots:**
1. An empty pumpkin bucket with a single wrapper. Text: *Someone stole ALL the candy.*
2. Kids in costumes with flashlights, finding clue cards in the dark.
3. Two teams (red and blue cards) racing to the same hiding spot.
4. Accusation time: the vampire-costumed grandparent confesses.
5. The candy is recovered. Cheering.

**Caption:** The Great Halloween Candy Heist 🎃 Set in your own house, with your family as the suspects. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #halloweenparty #halloweengames #halloweenwithkids #partyideas

---

## 5. “Setup in 10 minutes” (for parents)

**Hook:** *10 minutes of setup = 1 hour of quiet-ish kids.*

**Shots (screen recording + real footage):**
1. Screen: typing kids’ and pets’ names into the builder (fast-forward).
2. Printer spitting out pages.
3. Scissors cutting clue cards.
4. Hiding cards: in a boot, under the doormat, behind the cereal box (quick cuts).
5. Kids arriving to find the mission letter.
6. A timer on screen: *57:00 later…* The kids present their accusation.

**Caption:** Free to try, no sign-up. Every case is logic-checked to have exactly one answer, so no “wait, that doesn’t make sense” moments.

**Hashtags:** #momhacks #kidsactivitiesathome #printables #boredkids

---

### Posting tips
- Post 3–5 times a week for the first month. One good video matters more than perfect editing.
- Reply to comments with short follow-up videos (“Here’s the code from yesterday’s video”).
- Seasonal timing: start Halloween videos in late September and Christmas videos in mid-November.
